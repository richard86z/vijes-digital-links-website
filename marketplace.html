BUYER & SELLER INFORMATION

Clear listings. Responsible selling.

Marketplace listings are submitted by independent sellers and are reviewed before publication. Sellers are responsible for providing accurate descriptions, genuine images they are authorised to use, clear prices, delivery terms and truthful information about product condition or service scope. Vijes may reject, pause or remove listings that are misleading, unlawful, infringing, unsafe or inconsistent with marketplace rules.

Before placing an order, review the seller's listing, currency, delivery charge, location, payment method and any stated conditions. Contact the seller or Vijes team when essential details are unclear. A listing is not a guarantee of stock, delivery time or product performance; those details should be confirmed before purchase.

Do not submit prohibited goods, counterfeit items, stolen property, misleading claims, personal data belonging to others or content that infringes intellectual-property rights. Buyers can contact Vijes through the website if a listing appears inaccurate or raises a safety concern.
