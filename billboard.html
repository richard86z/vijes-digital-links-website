ADVERTISING QUESTIONS

Before you advertise.

Clear information for businesses comparing online advertising options.

Where can I advertise my products online?

Options include search ads, social platforms, online marketplaces, relevant publications and business websites. Vijes Digital Links offers paid placements on this website through the Billboard request process.

How do I place an advert with Vijes?

Submit the request form with your advert details, preferred placement and campaign period. We review the request and send a custom quotation.

How much does website advertising cost?

Pricing is quoted individually based on placement, duration, creative requirements and availability. No payment is requested until the quotation and terms are confirmed.

When will my advert be published?

After you accept the terms, payment is independently verified and the advert is approved. The campaign is then scheduled for the agreed dates.

Advertising placements do not guarantee a particular number of views, clicks, leads or sales. Ask us for the campaign details available before making a decision.
