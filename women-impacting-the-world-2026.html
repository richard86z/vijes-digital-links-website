COMPLETED CLIENT PROJECT · EVENT MARKETING · GRAPHIC DESIGN

Celebrating women creating impact — and helping their message reach further.

How Vijes Digital Links supported Women Impacting the World Foundation's 2026 Annual Award Ceremony and Conference through promotional flyer design, advertising and marketing support.

CLIENTWomen Impacting the World Foundation
EVENT DATEFriday, 20 November 2026
VENUECSDA, near Crispan Gate, Yingi Rayfield

About the project

Women Impacting the World Foundation brought people together for its 2026 Annual Award Ceremony and Conference under the theme “Manifesting God's Glory.” The event recognised women of purpose and impact — including women who have built something for themselves, developed their communities, created businesses and contributed positively to society.

Vijes Digital Links supported the organisation with event advertising and marketing promotion and designed two promotional flyer concepts to help communicate the event's identity, theme and essential information.

What Vijes Digital Links delivered

  • Designed two event promotional flyer variations.
  • Presented the event theme, host, speakers and programme details in clear promotional layouts.
  • Supported advertising and marketing promotion for the event.
  • Helped the organisation communicate the event's purpose and invite attention to its celebration of women's leadership, enterprise and community impact.

The creative direction

The promotional designs used an elegant awards-inspired visual direction, with prominent event titles, thematic messaging, speaker information and key event details. The aim was to make the information easy to recognise while reflecting the celebratory nature of the occasion.

The event's sponsorship and supporters artwork was created separately by the Foundation and included Vijes Digital Links among the supporting organisations.

Artwork credit: The two event promotional flyer designs were created by Vijes Digital Links. The separate “Global Women of Impact Awards 2026 — Proudly Sponsored and Supported By” artwork was designed by Women Impacting the World Foundation and is shown only as recognition of the agency's support.

A client success story

With approval from the event host, Mrs Favour Kutdang, Vijes Digital Links is sharing the following client-approved project testimonial.

“Organising the Women Impacting the World Annual Award Ceremony and Conference 2026 was an important opportunity to celebrate women who are creating businesses, developing themselves, empowering communities and making meaningful contributions to society.

For an event of this importance, communicating our vision and reaching the right audience through effective advertising and marketing promotion was essential.

Vijes Digital Links supported our event with promotional graphic design, event flyer development and advertising and marketing support. Their work helped us present the event theme, communicate important programme information and create promotional materials that reflected the purpose and identity of the occasion.

We appreciated the creative support and the attention given to presenting our event professionally. We recognise Vijes Digital Links as one of the organisations that supported the promotion of the Women Impacting the World Annual Award Ceremony and Conference 2026.

We look forward to more opportunities for collaboration and continued impact.”

Host, Women Impacting the World Foundation
