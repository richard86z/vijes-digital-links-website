INFORMATION & PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Vijes Digital Links respects the privacy of people who contact us. This notice explains how information shared through our website enquiry form and direct contact channels is handled.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact us, you may provide your name, organisation, email address, the service you are interested in and details about your project. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the enquiry form.

How the enquiry form works

The project enquiry form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp so you can review and send it yourself. The form does not submit information to or store it on this website. If you choose to send the message, WhatsApp and its provider process it under their own terms and privacy practices. You can also contact us directly by email.

Why we use your information

We use information you send to respond to your enquiry, understand the requested work, communicate with you and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal or provide services. We do not sell your enquiry details.

Advertising and cookies

Vijes Digital Links may apply to use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, on informational pages. If advertising is enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display, measure and personalise advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable settings. We will update this notice if advertising or analytics technologies are introduced. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and review Google's advertising and privacy information before interacting with personalised ads. At present, this notice does not mean that advertisements are already running on this website.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as server logs and connection data, as part of operating and securing its service. Please review GitHub's privacy information for details of its processing.

Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a business relationship, meet applicable legal requirements or resolve disputes. You may contact us to ask about information you have shared with us or to request correction or deletion, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com.
