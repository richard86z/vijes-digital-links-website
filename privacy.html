INFORMATION & PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Vijes Digital Links respects the privacy of people who contact us. This notice explains how information shared through our website enquiry form and direct contact channels is handled.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact us, you may provide your name, organisation, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, website or social media link, the service you are interested in, your preferred start timeframe, preferred contact method and details about your project. The phone number, website link, timeframe and contact preference fields are optional. The form also offers a separate optional marketing-updates choice. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the enquiry form.

How the enquiry form works

When you submit the website enquiry form, the information you provide is transmitted to a Google Apps Script service operated by Vijes Digital Links, recorded in a private Google spreadsheet controlled by our business account and used to send an enquiry notification to vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com. The spreadsheet is restricted to authorised account access and is not publicly shared. Google may process the information to provide its Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail services under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Advertising enquiries

If you request a paid advertising placement, the information you submit is stored in the Vijes Ads Manager for review by authorised Vijes Digital Links personnel. We use it to assess the proposed advert, contact you, prepare a quotation, record payment-verification and approval status, schedule the campaign and report basic advert impressions and clicks. Advertiser contact details and quotation records are not displayed in the public advert feed. Do not include payment-card details, passwords or confidential customer information. Payments are not collected by the request form.

Marketplace orders and browser storage

If you place an order through the marketplace, the checkout form collects the information needed to submit and manage that order, including your name, email address, telephone number, country, city, delivery address, postal code, delivery note and the items ordered. This information is sent to the marketplace service operated for Vijes Digital Links so the order can be created, routed to the relevant seller and its status managed. The marketplace currently offers Cash on Delivery only; it does not collect online card payments through the marketplace checkout.

The marketplace uses your browser's local storage to remember basket contents and order-tracking tokens on that device. This helps you return to your basket and view order information. If you clear browser storage, change devices or use a different browser, these locally stored details may no longer be available. Do not use a shared device to store order-tracking information unless you can protect access to it.

Marketplace orders are processed by the marketplace's hosting and application service provider. Seller access to order details should be limited to information needed to fulfil the relevant order. We are confirming the applicable backend retention and deletion arrangements and will update this notice if the processing arrangements change. For an order-related privacy request, contact vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com and include the order reference, not payment credentials.

Why we use your information

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, understand the work you need, communicate with you and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal or provide services. We do not sell your enquiry details.

Optional marketing updates

The form includes a separate, optional checkbox for people who want to receive occasional marketing insights, service news and company updates. It is not selected by default. We record whether you opted in and when. Submitting an enquiry does not by itself subscribe you to marketing. We will establish and honour an unsubscribe and suppression process before sending marketing campaigns. You can also contact vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com to withdraw your permission.

Retention, access and your choices

We retain enquiry records only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a business relationship, meet applicable legal requirements or resolve disputes. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, subject to applicable legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to restrict access to enquiry records, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense code is installed on selected informational pages while this website is being reviewed for advertising. At the time of this notice, the site has not yet been approved to show AdSense advertisements. If and when ads are served, Google LLC and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to collect or receive information from your browser or device. These technologies may be used to display, measure and personalise advertisements based on your visits to this website and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and/or other websites. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also visit YourAdChoices to learn about opting out of some third-party vendors' personalised advertising. Browser settings may also allow you to manage or delete cookies.

Additional advertising vendors may be used if enabled in our advertising account. The website does not currently display a cookie-consent banner or an integrated consent management platform. Before enabling personalised advertising for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Vijes Digital Links must configure an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework, or ensure that advertising is not served in a way that requires that consent signal.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as server logs and connection data, as part of operating and securing its service. Please review GitHub's privacy information for details of its processing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com.
