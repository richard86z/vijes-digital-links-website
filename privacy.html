INFORMATION, COOKIES & ADVERTISING

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Vijes Digital Links respects the privacy of people who visit our website, contact our team, submit an advertising request or use the Vijes Marketplace. This notice explains the information we handle, why we use it, the services that help us operate the website and how you can contact us about your information.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Vijes Digital Links is responsible for the information collected through this website and the business services described here. For privacy questions or requests, email vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive identity documents through ordinary email or website enquiry forms.

2. Information you may provide

Depending on how you interact with us, you may provide your name, organisation, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, country or location, website or social-media link, service interest, project description, campaign details, preferred dates, delivery address, order information or messages sent to our team. Some fields are optional. Please provide only the information needed for your enquiry, order or campaign request.

When you visit the website, your browser or device may also provide technical information to our hosting, security and advertising service providers. This may include an IP address, browser and device details, referring page, approximate location derived from the connection, page activity, cookie identifiers and information about interactions with adverts.

3. Website enquiries and communication

When you submit the main website enquiry form, the information is sent to a Google Apps Script service operated for Vijes Digital Links, recorded in a private Google spreadsheet controlled by our business account and used to notify our team at vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com. Google may process the information to provide its Apps Script, Sheets and email services. We use enquiry information to understand your request, respond, ask follow-up questions and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal or provide agreed services.

The enquiry form has a separate, optional marketing-updates checkbox. It is not selected by default. Sending an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing messages. Where you choose to opt in, we use that permission for the updates described beside the checkbox. You can withdraw permission by using an unsubscribe method included in a message or by emailing us.

4. Advertising and Billboard requests

When you submit an advertising request, we may process your business and contact details, advert description, creative files or links, placement preference, target audience, requested campaign duration and destination URL. The request is reviewed so that we can check suitability and availability, clarify the scope, prepare a personalised quotation and manage approval, payment verification, scheduling and campaign reporting.

Submitting an advert request is not a confirmed booking. Payment details such as full card numbers or security codes are not requested through the advert enquiry form. Advertiser contact details and quotation records are used for campaign administration and are not intended to be displayed in the public advert feed. The advertising-management application and its hosting providers may process the information needed to operate that service.

5. Marketplace orders and browser storage

If you place an order through the Vijes Marketplace, the checkout may collect your name, email address, telephone number, country, city, delivery address, postal code, delivery note and the products or services requested. The information is processed by the marketplace application so an order can be created, managed and, where applicable, shared with the relevant seller for fulfilment and order-related communication.

The Marketplace currently supports Cash on Delivery for eligible listings. Its website checkout does not currently collect online card payments. Confirm the seller, product, final amount, delivery charge and delivery arrangements before submitting an order.

The Marketplace uses browser local storage to remember basket contents and order-tracking tokens on the device you use. Clearing browser storage, changing devices or using another browser may remove those locally stored details. Avoid saving order-tracking information on a shared device that other people can access. Marketplace hosting and application providers may process order information to operate the service. We limit access to information according to the operational need to manage the order; relevant sellers may receive the order details they need to fulfil an order.

6. Why we use information and when it is shared

We use information to answer enquiries, deliver or administer requested services, prepare quotations, manage advertising campaigns, process marketplace orders, provide customer support, protect the website, prevent misuse, maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell website enquiry details as a mailing list.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the website and business workflows, including Google services used for forms, spreadsheets and email; GitHub Pages for website hosting; and hosted application services used for the Marketplace and advertising-management tools. We share only information relevant to the service being provided. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect rights or safety, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

Links to external websites, stores, payment services or third-party platforms are governed by the privacy notices of those providers. Review their terms and privacy information before submitting information or making a payment on an external service.

7. Retention, correction and deletion

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected. Enquiry records are retained while we respond to and manage the request and for any reasonable follow-up, business-record, legal or dispute-resolution need. Advertising records are retained as needed to assess requests, document quotations and approvals, administer campaigns and resolve campaign or payment questions. Marketplace order records are retained as needed to fulfil orders, handle customer or seller queries, address returns or disputes where applicable, and meet accounting or legal obligations.

We periodically review records and delete or anonymise information when it is no longer required, unless a lawful business or legal reason requires it to be kept. You may email us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to ask us to stop using it for a particular purpose. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law or a legitimate record-keeping obligation requires it. We will explain any relevant limitation when responding.

8. Cookies, Google AdSense and advertising technologies

Selected pages on this website contain the Google AdSense advertising tag. Whether adverts are displayed depends on Google's site approval and ad-serving status. When Google ads are served, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, browser or device identifiers and similar technologies to deliver, measure, limit repetition of, and help personalise advertising. These technologies may allow advertising to be based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

For information about how Google uses data when people use partner websites or apps, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage Google personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also visit YourAdChoices to learn about choices for some participating third-party vendors. Browser settings may also allow you to block or delete cookies, although some website functions may not work as expected.

Where personalised Google advertising is served to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Vijes Digital Links must complete the appropriate consent-message configuration in AdSense before enabling personalised advertising for visitors in those regions. The consent experience and available choices depend on the visitor's location and the configuration made in the relevant advertising service.

Some United States state privacy laws also give eligible residents choices about targeted advertising and the sale or sharing of personal information. Vijes Digital Links must configure the applicable United States state-regulations message and geographic coverage in AdSense Privacy & messaging, and honour applicable opt-out choices and preference signals. The available controls depend on the visitor's location and the advertising settings in force.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to restrict access to business records and use service settings intended to protect information. No website, email transmission, browser storage or online service can be guaranteed completely secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe information you sent to Vijes Digital Links has been exposed or used improperly.

10. Changes to this notice

We may revise this notice when our website, applications, providers or information-handling practices change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page. Please check this page periodically.

Contact
