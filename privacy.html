INFORMATION & PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Vijes Digital Links respects the privacy of people who contact us. This notice explains how information shared through our website enquiry form and direct contact channels is handled.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact us, you may provide your name, organisation, email address, the service you are interested in and details about your project. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the enquiry form.

How the enquiry form works

When you submit the website enquiry form, the information you provide is transmitted to a Google Apps Script service operated by Vijes Digital Links, recorded in a private Google spreadsheet controlled by our business account and used to send an enquiry notification to vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com. The spreadsheet is restricted to authorised account access and is not publicly shared. Google may process the information to provide its Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail services under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Advertising enquiries

If you request a paid advertising placement, the information you choose to include in the request is placed into an email addressed to Vijes Digital Links through your own email application. The request is delivered only if you send that email. We use the information to assess the proposed advert, communicate with you, prepare a quotation and administer an approved campaign. Do not include payment-card details or confidential information.

Why we use your information

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, understand the work you need, communicate with you and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal or provide services. We do not sell your enquiry details.

Optional marketing updates

The form includes a separate, optional checkbox for people who want to receive occasional marketing insights, service news and company updates. It is not selected by default. We record whether you opted in and when. Submitting an enquiry does not by itself subscribe you to marketing. We will establish and honour an unsubscribe and suppression process before sending marketing campaigns. You can also contact vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com to withdraw your permission.

Retention, access and your choices

We retain enquiry records only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a business relationship, meet applicable legal requirements or resolve disputes. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, subject to applicable legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to restrict access to enquiry records, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Advertising and cookies

Vijes Digital Links may apply to use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, on informational pages. If advertising is enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display, measure and personalise advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable settings. We will update this notice if advertising or analytics technologies are introduced. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and review Google's advertising and privacy information before interacting with personalised ads. At present, this notice does not mean that advertisements are already running on this website.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as server logs and connection data, as part of operating and securing its service. Please review GitHub's privacy information for details of its processing.

Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a business relationship, meet applicable legal requirements or resolve disputes. You may contact us to ask about information you have shared with us or to request correction or deletion, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact vijesdigitallinks@gmail.com.
