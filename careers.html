CAREERS, FREELANCE & COLLABORATION

Build useful work with Vijes.

Vijes Digital Links welcomes conversations with capable people who can contribute to digital marketing, creative production, research, technology and business-support projects. We work with organisations across markets and may build project teams around the requirements of a particular brief.

Current opportunities

This page is an ongoing expression-of-interest channel. It is not a list of confirmed vacancies, and no specific salaried role or contract is currently being advertised here. We publish or discuss an opportunity only when a project requirement, scope and engagement arrangement have been identified.

Areas where we may collaborate

  • Marketing and communications: digital marketing, paid-campaign support, social media planning, email marketing, content writing, copy editing and campaign reporting.
  • Creative and design: graphic design, brand assets, presentation design, promotional materials, image preparation and creative production.
  • Web, software and automation: website design and development, quality checks, workflow mapping, no-code tools, software support and practical automation.
  • Research and business development: desk research, market and competitor reviews, survey planning, data organisation, prospect research and partnership research.
  • Language services: translation, interpretation coordination, multilingual content review and localisation support.
  • Project and operations support: project coordination, administrative support, documentation, data handling and carefully scoped outsourcing.

Ways to work together

Depending on an actual project, collaboration may be a defined freelance assignment, a short-term specialist contribution, a service-provider partnership or a longer project arrangement. The scope, deliverables, deadline, fees, payment terms, confidentiality requirements and ownership of work must be discussed and agreed in writing before work begins. An expression of interest does not create an employment relationship or guarantee paid work.

What to include in your profile

Send a short introduction, your area of specialisation, location and time zone, relevant experience, availability, preferred type of engagement and links to examples of work where available. You may include a CV or professional profile. Do not send passwords, bank-card information or sensitive personal documents at the first enquiry stage.

How we review expressions of interest

We review submissions for relevance to the work we may need, the quality of examples provided, communication, availability and the requirements of the project. We may contact you for clarification or a discussion when a suitable need arises. We cannot promise an individual response to every unsolicited profile, and submitting information does not guarantee selection.

Fair and responsible collaboration

We value accurate representation of skills, reliable communication, respect for client confidentiality, appropriate permissions for submitted work samples and professional conduct. Do not include confidential client materials in a portfolio unless you have permission to share them. Any client-facing work must follow the agreed scope and quality checks.
