Vijes Digital Billboard

A dedicated online billboard experience designed to present promotional adverts with a clear visual layout and essential advertiser information.

The advertiser journey is designed around individual quotations rather than public fixed prices: advertisers select a format and duration, receive a quote by email, and move to payment verification and creative approval before publication. The campaign period begins when the advert goes live. The project measures observable activity such as impressions and clicks without presenting those signals as guaranteed leads or sales.

Project typeVijes-owned advertising platform
Work coveredPublic billboard page and connected advert-management experience.
PurposeGive businesses a digital placement to showcase their promotions and direct interested visitors to their websites.
