HOW A PARTNERSHIP ENQUIRY WORKS

A clear process from first conversation to delivery.

Every proposal is considered on its own merits. An enquiry does not automatically create a partnership, reserve capacity or authorise work to begin.

STEP 01

Share the opportunity

Tell us about your organisation, the challenge or opportunity, the audience or market and the outcome you want to achieve.

STEP 02

Review and discuss

We review fit, capability, timing, resources and any due-diligence or compliance requirements that apply.

STEP 03

Agree the scope

Where there is a fit, both parties clarify responsibilities, deliverables, milestones, fees, data handling and written terms.

STEP 04

Deliver and review

Work proceeds after agreement, with communication and progress reviews aligned to the approved project plan.

OUR COLLABORATION PRINCIPLES

Professional relationships built on clarity and trust.

We aim to make expectations clear before work begins. We will discuss what Vijes can contribute, what a partner is expected to provide and how the work will be reviewed. We do not present a proposed collaboration as an existing agreement or promise outcomes that have not been demonstrated.

Clear responsibilities

Define ownership, decision-making, deliverables and communication contacts.

Transparent terms

Confirm scope, timeframes, fees, payment arrangements and approval before delivery.

Responsible information handling

Share only information needed for the project and agree appropriate access and handling.

Evidence-led reporting

Use agreed indicators and available evidence to review progress without inventing results.
